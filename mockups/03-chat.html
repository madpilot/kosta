The lower leaves on my Sungold are going yellow. Is that nitrogen?
K
Sprout Agent

Probably not nitrogen — at day 64 on a Sungold, lower leaves yellowing is usually just the plant shedding old foliage as it puts energy into the upper trusses. It's normal, and a little tidy.

But two things would change my answer:

  1. If the yellowing is spreading upward fast — that's likely nitrogen or magnesium.
  2. If the leaves are also curling or speckled — could be early blight starting.
